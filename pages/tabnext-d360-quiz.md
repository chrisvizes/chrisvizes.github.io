Questions on Tableau Next, Tableau Semantics and Data 360, referencing the Salesforce documentation. Pick a short set or sit a full paper, answer in whatever style the question calls for, and get a score at the end.

Get one wrong and you get the sentence from the documentation that settles it, plus a link to the page it came from. That is the point of the thing: the wrong answers should teach you more than the right ones.

## Where the questions come from

Every question is tied to a verbatim sentence on a Salesforce help page, and a script checks that the quoted sentence really appears there before the question is allowed into the bank. If a question looks wrong you can follow the link and see for yourself.

Some questions rest on a fact the documentation states only in passing, inside a Note, a table, or step 7 of a procedure. Those carry a **buried** badge and are scored separately at the end, because knowing them tends to mean you have actually used the product rather than
read about it.

Anything touching a Beta or Pilot feature carries a **beta** badge. Those move, so check the live page for up-to-date information.
